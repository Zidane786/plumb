## DS-YYYYMMDD-slug · Short title of the decision
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Decided by: name
- Options: option one, option two ([directions](directions.html))
- Chosen: the option picked
- Why: one or two sentences, in the owner's terms.
- Proposals: [variants](prototype/proposals/<name>/index.html)
- Screens: screen-id, other-screen-id (or "all")
- Ticket: optional ticket id
